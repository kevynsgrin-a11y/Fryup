# Fryup.UK — Photography & Visual Asset Direction

## Aesthetic Guidelines
- **Lighting**: Warm 45-degree morning window light with soft directional shadows.
- **Environment**: Dark oak, warm stone counters, cream enamelware plates, cast-iron skillets, linen napkins.
- **Food Texture**: Realistic texture—glistening egg yolks, crispy bacon edges, rendered sausage casings, caramelised tomatoes, rich dark black pudding crust.
- **Composition**: 3:2 or 4:3 aspect ratios for desktop heroes, 4:5 for mobile, 1:1 for ingredient cards. 50mm or 85mm lens feel with natural depth of field.
- **Strict Exclusions**: No fake text embedded in images, no cool blue casts, no oversaturated plastic-looking food, no floating utensils, no anatomical deformities in hands, no extra random ingredients not in the recipe.

---

## Asset Generation & Derivatives Pipeline
1. Master generation (min 2400px long edge).
2. Saved to `public/images/recipes/[slug].jpg`.
3. WebP & AVIF responsive derivatives created for 360px, 768px, 1200px viewports.
4. Alt text & caption assigned for WCAG compliance.
