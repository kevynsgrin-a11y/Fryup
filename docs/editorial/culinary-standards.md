# Fryup.UK — Culinary Standards & Formulation Policy

## 1. Source Hierarchy & Originality
Every recipe published on Fryup.UK is an original synthesis derived from multi-tier culinary references and verified kitchen mechanics.

- **Tier 1 (Primary)**: Official food standards organisations (UK FSA), food science literature (Harold McGee, Heston Blumenthal chemistry principles), historical archival records (Food in England by Dorothy Hartley).
- **Tier 2 (Professional)**: Classic British cookbooks (Delia Smith, Elizabeth David, Jane Grigson, Rick Stein), recognised culinary institutes (Leiths, City & Guilds hospitality curricula).
- **Tier 3 (Trade & Specialty)**: Master butchers' guides, British Pig Association standards, Traditional Black Pudding Association guides.
- **Tier 4 (Cultural & Community)**: Regional café surveys, traditional working-class breakfast preferences.

**Plagiarism Ban**: No recipe text, ingredient ratios, or distinctive prose may be copied verbatim from third-party sources.

---

## 2. Classification System
Every recipe on Fryup.UK must be explicitly tagged with one of six classifications:

1. `classic-full-plate`: Traditional complete fry-ups cooked on stovetop, oven, or griddle.
2. `regional-tradition`: Authentic regional specialties (Scottish, Welsh, Cornish, Ulster).
3. `component-technique`: Master recipes for individual items (bacon, eggs, sausages, pudding, beans, potatoes, bread).
4. `dietary-adaptation`: Verified vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free variations.
5. `modern-interpretation`: Contemporary formats (wraps, muffins, hashes, pizzas, benedicts, scotch eggs).
6. `condiments-beverages`: Sauces, chutneys, and hot tea preparations.

---

## 3. Structural Recipe Elements
Every recipe file must supply:
- Canonical metric quantities (`g`, `ml`, `cm`, `°C` fan).
- Exact prep, cook, rest, active, and total elapsed durations.
- Equipment checklist (pan diameter, cast iron vs non-stick, oven tray size).
- Sensory doneness cues (e.g. "whisked eggs form soft curds that hold their shape", "sausage casing renders golden brown with internal temp of 75°C").
- Timing integration notes for adding the component into a complete fry-up.
- Safe storage, cooling, reheating, and leftover guidance.
