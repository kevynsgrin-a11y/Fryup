# Fryup.UK — Allergen & Dietary Tagging Standard

## UK 14 Major Allergens Matrix

Every recipe published on Fryup.UK must audit and explicitly tag the presence or absence of the 14 mandatory UK allergens:

1. **Celery**: Celery stalks, leaves, seeds, celery salt.
2. **Cereals containing Gluten**: Wheat, rye, barley, oats (spelt, kamut). Present in fried bread, black pudding, sausages (rusk), brown sauce, toast.
3. **Crustaceans**: Crabs, lobster, prawns. (Checked in seafood breakfast adaptations).
4. **Eggs**: Present in fried, poached, scrambled eggs, crumpet batters, scotch eggs, baps.
5. **Fish**: Present in kippers, Worcestershire sauce (anchovies), devilled kidneys.
6. **Lupin**: Lupin flour in baked goods.
7. **Milk**: Butter, cream, cheese, milk used in scrambled eggs, mash, mashed potatoes.
8. **Molluscs**: Mussels, snails, squid, cockles (Welsh breakfast).
9. **Mustard**: Mustard powder, liquid mustard in devilled kidneys, chutneys, sausage glazes.
10. **Nuts**: Almonds, hazelnuts, walnuts, pecans.
11. **Peanuts**: Peanut oils or pastes.
12. **Sesame**: Sesame seeds on baps or rolls.
13. **Soya**: Soy lecithin, soy proteins in plant bacon, vegan sausages, processed beans.
14. **Sulphites**: Sulphur dioxide in commercial sausages, wine reductions, dried fruit chutneys.

---

## Dietary Classification Guidelines

- `Vegetarian`: Contains no meat, fish, slaughter by-products (no gelatin, no animal lard, no traditional black pudding). Milk, cheese, butter, and Lion mark eggs allowed.
- `Vegan`: Contains zero animal products (no meat, fish, eggs, dairy, butter, honey, lard, gelatin).
- `Gluten-Free`: Requires certified gluten-free sausages (no wheat rusk), gluten-free black pudding, gluten-free bread, and gluten-free tamari/sauces.
- `Dairy-Free`: Uses vegetable oils, lard, or plant milks instead of butter/cream/milk.
