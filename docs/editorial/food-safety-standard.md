# Fryup.UK — UK Food Safety & Temperature Standard

## Overview
All food safety guidance on Fryup.UK is aligned with current **UK Food Standards Agency (FSA)** recommendations and public health regulations.

---

## 1. Internal Meat Temperatures

| Ingredient / Item | Safe Minimum Internal Temperature | Note / Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Pork Sausages / Chipolatas | **75 °C** | Hold for at least 30 seconds. Juices must run clear with no pink meat remaining. |
| British Back Bacon | **70 °C** | Fat rendered, meat opaque and fully cooked throughout. |
| Black Pudding / Hog's Pudding | **75 °C** | Reheat thoroughly if pre-cooked; cook raw blood puddings to 75°C core temp. |
| Gammon Steak / Kidneys | **75 °C** | Kidneys cooked until firm and hot to center; gammon tender and fully heated. |

---

## 2. Egg Safety Guidance (UK Lion Mark)
- **Lion Code Eggs**: In the UK, eggs bearing the British Lion Quality Mark may be eaten raw or runny by vulnerable groups (pregnant women, elderly, infants).
- **Non-Lion / Import Eggs**: Must be cooked until both yolk and white are solid (internal temp 70°C+).
- **Cross-Contamination**: Raw egg shells and liquid egg must be handled with clean utensils. Wash hands immediately after cracking eggs.

---

## 3. Cooling, Storage & Reheating

- **Cooling Window**: Cool hot breakfast leftovers (beans, sausage, potatoes, rice) to room temperature within **90 minutes** before refrigerating.
- **Refrigeration**: Store leftovers in airtight containers at **4 °C or lower**. Consume within 2 days (48 hours).
- **Reheating Core Temp**: Reheat thoroughly until piping hot throughout, reaching a minimum internal core temperature of **75 °C**. Never reheat cooked food more than once.
- **Rice / Grains**: High-risk for *Bacillus cereus*. Cool rapidly and consume within 24 hours.

---

## 4. Cross-Contamination & Allergen Declarations
- Use separate cutting boards for raw pork sausages/bacon (red board) and cooked/ready-to-eat items (yellow/white board).
- Explicitly disclose the 14 major UK allergens on every recipe: Gluten, Crustaceans, Eggs, Fish, Peanuts, Soybeans, Milk, Nuts, Celery, Mustard, Sesame, Sulphites, Lupin, Molluscs.
