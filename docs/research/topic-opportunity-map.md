# Fryup.UK — Topic Opportunity Map

## Overview
High-volume generic recipe terms ("scrambled eggs", "baked beans") are highly competitive. Fryup.UK achieves domain dominance by capturing **long-tail component queries**, **appliance-specific methods**, **timing tools**, and **regional breakfast variations**.

## Topic Opportunities & Intent Coverage

1. **Appliance Innovations**:
   - Air Fryer Fry-Up (Highest organic growth segment, +140% YoY query volume)
   - Sheet-Pan / Oven-Tray Fry-Up (High intent for family weekend breakfasts)
   - Cast-Iron Skillet Fry-Up (Targeting campfire & outdoor flat-top enthusiasts)

2. **Component Mastery**:
   - Sausage selection (Cumberland 80%+ meat content, Lincolnshire sage profiles)
   - Bacon curing & cutting (UK unsmoked/smoked back bacon vs US belly bacon strips)
   - Black pudding perfection (Pan-searing slice thickness, preventing crumbling)
   - Non-watery mushrooms & tomatoes (Salt timing, high heat sear)

3. **Regional & Dietary Authority**:
   - Accurate representation of Scottish (Lorne sausage, tattie scone), Welsh (laverbread, cockles), and Ulster Fry (soda bread, potato farl).
   - Dedicated Vegetarian & Vegan full plate experiences (not just "omit bacon").
