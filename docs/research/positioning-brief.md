# Fryup.UK — Positioning Brief

## Core Brand Identity
- **Primary Brand Line**: “The proper fry-up, made at home.”
- **Canonical Domain**: `https://fryup.uk`
- **Voice & Tone**: Authoritative, warm, precise, confident, authentic British without forced gimmickry.
- **Editorial Mission**: To elevate the English breakfast from an afterthought to a masterclass in timing, quality sourcing, and culinary perfection.

---

## 10 Brand Commandments
1. **Timing is King**: No component leaves a guest waiting. We equip cooks with precise, coordinated timelines.
2. **Metric First**: All ingredients state exact metric units (`g`, `ml`, `°C` fan).
3. **British Terminology**: Back bacon, baps, brown sauce, chipolatas, black pudding, kippers, and frying pans.
4. **No Artificial Hype**: Never call a dish "game-changing" or "mouth-watering" without backing it up with culinary logic.
5. **Authentic Photography**: Photorealistic editorial morning light photography depicting the actual dish. No stock placeholders.
6. **No Intrusive Ads**: Clean typography, fast LCP, zero pop-ups above the fold.
7. **Transparent Standards**: Explicit distinction between historical tradition and modern adaptation.
8. **Inclusivity Without Shaming**: High-grade vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free full plates given equal craft and respect.
9. **FSA Food Safety**: Strict temperature control notes for pork, eggs, cooling, and reheating.
10. **Zero Fabricated Claims**: No fake chef bios, fake award badges, or unverified test kitchen numbers.
