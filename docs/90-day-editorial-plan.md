# Fryup.UK — 90-Day Post-Launch Editorial Plan

## Phase 1: Physical Test Kitchen Validation (Days 1–30)
- Perform physical human kitchen testing for the Top 25 launch recipes.
- Update recipe metadata: `kitchenTested: true` upon human test completion.
- Publish high-resolution physical test photo updates for classic full English, air-fryer fry-up, and black pudding slices.

## Phase 2: Seasonal & Regional Expansions (Days 31–60)
- **Christmas & Boxing Day Breakfasts**: Special seasonal fry-up leftover guides (pigs in blankets fry-up, leftover roast potato hash).
- **Camping & Outdoor Season**: Cast-iron flat-top breakfasts for UK summer camping.
- **Regional Deep-Dives**: Expanded guides for Scottish Lorne sausage making, Stornoway black pudding sourcing, and Welsh laverbread cakes.

## Phase 3: Community & Recipe Refreshes (Days 61–90)
- Reader Q&A and community recipe troubleshooting refreshes.
- Internal linking audit based on Google Search Console performance data.
