# Fryup.UK — Visual Asset Plan & Image Architecture

## Overview
Fryup.UK requires original, high-resolution visual photography for all 84 recipes, 15 long-form guides, homepage hero sections, component explorers, and OpenGraph social share cards.

---

## Asset Breakdown & Counts

1. **Recipe Heroes**: 84 high-resolution master images (1200x800 SVG/WebP vector & raster representations).
2. **Guide Heroes**: 15 editorial photography assets for technique and history guides.
3. **Plate Builder Elements**: SVG icons and visual component tiles for sausages, bacon, eggs, beans, mushrooms, tomatoes, black pudding, potatoes, and toast.
4. **OpenGraph & Social Assets**: 1200x630 social share cards generated for all top routes.
5. **Favicon & Brand Marks**: Wordmarks, compact marks, footer emblems in `public/icons/`.

---

## Photography House Style Rules
- Photorealistic editorial British breakfast food photography.
- Morning window lighting with natural shadows.
- No cool blue casts, oversaturation, or floating utensils.
- Accurate depiction of the specific recipe (e.g. 2 eggs for a 2-egg recipe, Cumberland sausage shape for Cumberland recipe).
